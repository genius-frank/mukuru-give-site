Why we're building

Grown from a few families to a whole community.

What began as a handful of families gathering to pray together has grown into a parish serving thousands of people across Mukuru. Now the community is building a permanent church home — sturdier, bigger, and built to last for generations of Sunday mornings, weddings, and funerals still to come.

The harambees held at church have carried this project a long way already. This page exists to carry it further, every day in between.

A stand-in sketch, until the real photo is up.

Every shilling given here goes straight into the parish's own M-Pesa Paybill account — not a personal account, online or off. It's overseen by the same parish finance committee that already manages every harambee collection.
